# Cloudy provider icon sources

The `curseforge.svg` asset is retrieved from the Simple Icons CDN (`https://cdn.simpleicons.org/curseforge`) and is distributed under the Simple Icons CC0 1.0 dedication, subject to the provider's own trademark rights. The catalog license is available at `https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md`.

The `custom.svg`, `modrinth.svg`, `ftb.svg`, `technic.svg`, and `import.svg` files are original neutral geometric symbols authored for Cloudy Launcher. They are intentionally not copies or recolors of provider trademarks. In particular, Modrinth's official frontend copying notice prohibits use of its branding elements without explicit written permission (`https://github.com/modrinth/code/blob/main/apps/frontend/COPYING.md`). Provider names remain visible as nominative labels for the real native installers.


## Provider references

The real native provider workflows remain associated with their official sites: Modrinth (`https://modrinth.com/`), CurseForge (`https://www.curseforge.com/`), Feed The Beast (`https://feed-the-beast.com/`), and Technic (`https://www.technicpack.net/`). These URLs were consulted for provider identity only; their logos were not copied into Cloudy except for the separate Simple Icons CurseForge asset noted above.
